Business question: is this a real problem, and what do shoppers say goes wrong?

What Kroger should do

Non-app loyalists

Auto-load top offers to the loyalty card. They won't open an app, and they capture the least.

Casual app users

Auto-apply eligible offers, plus a wrong-size warning at the scanner and real-time sync for last-minute clips.

Every failure

Credit a failed coupon automatically to the next order. The service desk recovers too little to protect trust.

App team

Fix clip, load and scan reliability first. It's the top real complaint, and no coupon logic helps if the app can't open the offer.
