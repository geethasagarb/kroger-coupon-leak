What Kroger could do better

Shoppers get only about half of the coupon savings they qualify for. Five fixes, ranked by where the money leaks, each tied to the evidence behind it.

FixThe problem todayExpected result
1Apply eligible offers automaticallyCasual app users first 36% of qualifying savings is never clipped, 3.4× bigger than checkout failures. Casual app users capture only 45%. A/B test: realization 83.3% → 87.3%, capture 61% → 82%; casual users 46% → 79%.
2Auto-load top offers to the loyalty cardNon-app loyalists They capture just 13%. With casual in-store users they hold 60% of all leaked dollars. Reaches the biggest untapped group without asking them to open an app.
3Sync last-minute clips and warn on wrong sizeIn-store checkout Clips made under 15 minutes before checkout fail 59% of the time (vs about 12%). Wrong size is the top failure at 32%. Removes the two largest in-store failure causes at the register.
4Credit failed coupons to the next orderEvery failure A first failure cuts later trips 5.4%, about $130k in sales, 8× the coupons' value. The service desk recovers only 7%. Protects repeat trips and takes the burden off the service desk.
5Fix clip, load and scan reliabilityApp team Top real complaint: 37% of coupon-failure reviews say the app won't clip, load or scan. Those reviews average 1.7★ vs the app's 4.4★. Every other fix depends on the app working first.
